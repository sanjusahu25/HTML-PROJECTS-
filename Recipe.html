Chocolate Cake

This cake is more than just a dessert; it represents my personal growth and passion for baking. When I started, I used to take almost 4 hours because of the hot weather and my lack of practice. Today, through patience and dedication, I can bake the sponge and finish the full cake decoration in under 1 hour. I am truly proud of myself and hope you enjoy this recipe!

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chocolate cake premix
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • A few drops of chocolate essence
  • A few drops of liquid food color
  • 1 cup Tropolite whipped cream
  • 1/2 cup melted chocolate or chocolate ganache
  • 3 tablespoons strawberry crush
  • 1/4 cup choco chips
  • Decorative sprinkles
  • Edible spray color for final touch

Instructions

  1. In a mixing bowl, combine 1 cup chocolate cake premix with water, oil, and chocolate essence.
  2. Mix gently until a smooth batter forms without any lumps.
  3. Pour the batter into a greased baking tin and bake at 180°C for 25 to 30 minutes until a toothpick inserted comes out clean.
  4. While the sponge cools down, whip the Tropolite cream in a chilled bowl until soft peaks form, adding a drop of food color if desired.
  5. Slice the cooled cake sponge horizontally into two layers.
  6. Soak the bottom layer with a little water, spread a layer of whipped Tropolite cream, and add a generous layer of strawberry crush and choco chips.
  7. Place the top sponge layer, then cover the entire cake evenly with the remaining whipped cream and pour melted chocolate on top.
  8. Decorate the cake with sprinkles, choco chips, and finish with a spray color coat for an attractive bakery-style sheen.
  9. Refrigerate for 30 minutes before slicing and serving.
